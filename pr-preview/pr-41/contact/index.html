Message us, or send your club logo

Use this page to write to Archery Calendars, or to send your club logo so it can appear on your events.

A square image works best (PNG, JPEG or WebP). After you send a logo we add it to your club’s events, usually within a day. This does not replace sending an event or a calendar: use New event or Send your calendar for those.

  1. Fill in your name, email and club name.
  2. Type your message, or choose your logo file, or both.
  3. Press Send. You will see a thank-you page when it has gone through.

So we can reply, and tell you when a logo is on your events.

PNG, JPEG or WebP, under 4 MB. A square logo looks best on the calendar.

Or email us instead

🔒 Your name, email, club, message and logo are emailed to us (nfshold@gmail.com, NFS Strategic Holdings) via FormSubmit. We only use them to reply and to put your logo on your events. We don’t publish your email or the message. Privacy
