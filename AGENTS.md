# Agents

Read these two files before changing this repository:

1. [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md) — where the work stopped, and the note to add before you stop.
2. [PROJECT_RULES.md](PROJECT_RULES.md) — what the site is, what must keep working, and how a change goes live.

[IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md) is the queue. Log a new idea there, and build one at a time.

Jarvis is the lead and the default builder. Claude, OpenAI, and Grok Bot may also ship code, as long as the change goes through a pull request, the `smoke` check is green, and a signed entry is added to HANDOFF.md (`[Jarvis]`, `[Claude]`, `[OpenAI]`, or `[Grok Bot]`). Nothing goes live without Nathe's own OK.
