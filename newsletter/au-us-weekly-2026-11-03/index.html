RECENT RESULTS

Australia: National Target and Para Championships, Hobart, October 19-24. Individual gold medals went to Seoyoon An (recurve women), Jai Crawley (recurve men), Vida Halimian (compound women), Matt McDougall (compound men), May Hu (barebow women) and Judah Holmes (barebow men). Compound archer Jono Milne took his third consecutive win in the 90m 1440 round, scoring 1401. Full recap from Archery Australia

USA: Para Pan American Championships, Lima, Peru. The US team came home with 10 medals, five of them gold. KJ Polish won three golds and set three championship records. Report from USA Archery

YOUTH OLYMPIC GAMES: DAKAR 2026

Archery at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games starts this week at Abdoulaye Wade Stadium, Diamniadio. Australia has Brock Penna and Ellen Martini (both recurve). The US has Aarya Arun and Ye-eun Whang. The ranking round is on November 6, 5:30 pm to 8:00 pm Perth time (4:30 am to 7:00 am US Eastern). Eliminations follow on November 7, the men's finals on November 8, the women's finals on November 9 and the mixed team event on November 10. The schedule may change.
