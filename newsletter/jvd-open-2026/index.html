What is the JVD Open? Nicholas D'Amour among the top qualifiers in Eindhoven

Saturday 21 November 2026

← All newsletters

Hi archers,

You've probably seen the JVD Open all over archery social media this week. It's one of the biggest Vegas-shoot-style indoor opens in Europe, run by JVD Archery as part of the Kings of Archery series, and this year's edition was held 13 to 15 November at the Indoor Sportcentrum in Eindhoven, Netherlands — recurve, compound and barebow, plus a trade fair and seminar program alongside the shooting. In past editions, JVD's own site has described the event as drawing more than 1,000 competitors from 40-plus countries, which is part of why it gets so much attention online.

THE FORMAT

The JVD Open qualifies archers over 60 arrows and 30 arrows, with "900" awards for perfect qualification scores, a Joker Round, and head-to-head finals shot on the Sunday. It's the kind of big, sociable indoor open that draws a serious international field without being a world championship.

TEAM USA IN THE QUALIFICATION STANDINGS

Recurve men qualification (Ianseo), not the final result — the head-to-head finals were shot after qualifying

Team USA's Nicholas D'Amour qualified 2nd in recurve men with 898 (75 Xs), just behind Federico Musolesi of Italy, also on 898 but with more Xs (79), and just ahead of the Netherlands' Willem Bakker, also 898 (80 Xs). Mauro Nespoli (Italy), Matías Grande (Mexico) and Jonathan Vetter (Germany) topped the qualification scores on 900. D'Amour is the only USA archer we could confirm in the qualification standings as published.

RESULTS

As of when we're writing this, no final results (who won each division) have been published anywhere we could find — not on the event's own results page, and not on Archery Calendars' listing for the event either. The official entry list and qualification scores are on Ianseo, and that's the place to check for the finals results once they're posted: https://ianseo.net/TourData/2026/29948/. We'll follow up with a results wrap once they're up.
