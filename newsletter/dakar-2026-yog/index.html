Dakar 2026: Brock Penna sixth and Ellen Martini seventh for Australia

Tuesday 10 November 2026

← All newsletters

Hi archers,

Australia's two archers at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games have finished the individual events at Abdoulaye Wade Stadium in Diamniadio, Senegal.

Brock Penna finished 6th in the under 18 men's recurve. He beat Mikolaj Stankiewicz of Poland 6-0 in the round of 16 and Khalifa Musabeh Alkaabi of the UAE 6-4 in the round of 8, then lost the quarterfinal 3-7 to Ismail Ezzeldin of Egypt.

Ellen Martini finished 7th in the under 18 women's recurve. She beat Alison Langley of Canada 6-4 in the round of 16 and Merle Edlich of Germany 6-4 in the round of 8, then lost the quarterfinal 0-6 to Dunya Yenihayat of Turkiye.

The mixed team of Martini and Penna, the 5th seeds, beat Switzerland 5-3 in the round of 8. The final results, including the mixed team and the medal winners, are in the 11 November newsletter: Dakar 2026: Australia's results and the medal winners.

Results are from the official Ianseo result books: Brock Penna and Ellen Martini. The event page is on Archery Calendars: Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games – Archery.

Congratulations to Brock and Ellen, and to everyone who got them there.
