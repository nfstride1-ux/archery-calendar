Dakar 2026: Australia's results and the medal winners

Wednesday 11 November 2026

← All newsletters

Hi archers,

Archery at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games finished on Tuesday 10 November at Abdoulaye Wade Stadium in Dakar, Senegal. There were three events, all recurve under 18: men's individual, women's individual and mixed team.

Australia's two archers, Brock Penna and Ellen Martini, both reached the quarter-finals on their own and again in the mixed team. Penna finished sixth in the men's event. Martini finished seventh in the women's event. Together they finished sixth in the mixed team.

AUSTRALIA: BROCK PENNA

Recurve under 18 men, 6th

Penna qualified 10th with 673 from the 72-arrow ranking round at 60 metres. In the first elimination match he beat Mikolaj Stankiewicz of Poland 6-0 (sets 28-25, 25-23, 29-24). In the last 16 he beat Khalifa Musabeh Alkaabi of the UAE 6-4 (27-26, 27-26, 28-30, 27-30, 28-27). In the quarter-final he lost 3-7 to Ismail Ezzeldin of Egypt (29-29, 30-27, 27-29, 27-29, 28-29). Ezzeldin went on to finish fourth.

AUSTRALIA: ELLEN MARTINI

Recurve under 18 women, 7th

Martini qualified 8th with 661. In the first elimination match she beat Alison Langley of Canada 6-4 (23-22, 26-26, 26-27, 27-27, 27-20). In the last 16 she beat Merle Edlich of Germany 6-4 (22-30, 25-25, 26-24, 29-29, 25-23). In the quarter-final she lost 0-6 to Dunya Yenihayat of Türkiye (25-28, 27-30, 27-30). Yenihayat went on to win the gold medal. Martini finished equal seventh with Nouray Elsayed of Egypt.

AUSTRALIA: MIXED TEAM

Martini and Penna, 6th

The Australian mixed team was the 5th seed after the ranking round, with a combined score of 1,334. They beat Switzerland (Alexandra Rossier and Noe Wenger) 5-3 in the last 16 (32-33, 38-36, 35-35, 36-35). In the quarter-final they lost 3-5 to the USA (Whang Ye-eun and Aarya Arun), 37-37, 37-37, 36-38, 36-36. The USA went on to win the gold medal. Australia finished equal sixth with Spain.

MEDAL WINNERS

Recurve under 18 men (Sunday 8 November)

  • Gold: Maël Lamouret (France), who beat Chen Pin-An (Chinese Taipei) 6-4 in the final (30-29, 28-28, 28-29, 30-28, 28-28)
  • Silver: Chen Pin-An (Chinese Taipei)
  • Bronze: Lee Jiho (Korea), who beat Ismail Ezzeldin (Egypt) 6-2 (29-29, 29-28, 30-27, 28-28)

Recurve under 18 women (Monday 9 November)

  • Gold: Dunya Yenihayat (Türkiye), who beat Whang Ye-eun (USA) 6-5 in a shoot-off after the sets finished 5-5 (28-26, 30-30, 28-29, 30-28, 27-29; shoot-off 10+ to 10)
  • Silver: Whang Ye-eun (USA)
  • Bronze: Sandra Perez Cumbreras (Spain), who beat Tabitha Ern Lin Yeo (Singapore) 6-4 (27-27, 28-27, 27-29, 28-28, 29-28)

Mixed team (Tuesday 10 November)

  • Gold: USA (Whang Ye-eun and Aarya Arun), who beat France 5-1 in the final (38-37, 38-36, 38-38)
  • Silver: France (Aurane Lapouge and Maël Lamouret)
  • Bronze: Korea (Kim Sumin and Lee Jiho), who beat Türkiye 6-0 (40-38, 40-39, 39-38)

LOOKING AHEAD

That was the last of the archery at Dakar. The Youth Olympic Games themselves run until Friday 13 November.
