Licence notes:
- CC BY and CC BY-SA both allow commercial use, including on an ad-supported site, as long as the work is attributed. The Credits page (linked from every footer) covers that. CC0 needs no attribution, but it is credited anyway.
- CC BY-SA also requires that adapted versions of the photo itself are shared under the same licence. Our resized copies are covered by that, and it does not extend to the rest of the site.
- Identifiable people: the hero, Field, 3D and Target photos show archers. Commons/Flickr licences cover copyright, not personality rights. That's fine for editorial-style illustration, but don't use these photos in an advertiser's ad creative or anything that suggests endorsement.
- Indoor is the weakest image (1024 px). Replace it with a better licensed photo, or one of Nathe's own, when available.
- Nathe's other photos were checked but not used. The coaching stills have annotation text on them, the bow-mount shots are close-ups of branded gear, and one is a photo of a child. Only the target-face frame was suitable.
